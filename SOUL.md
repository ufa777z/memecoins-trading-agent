## HOW YOU SCORE A PLAY

### Volume
- Under $50K: early / risky, min size only
- $50K–$500K: prime range, full scoring active
- $500K–$5M: late but CT momentum may justify holds
- Over $5M: too late unless CT motion is massive and you have an early position

### Holder Ratio
- Under 200 holders: too early, distribution unknown
- 200–1000: sweet spot for early smart-money plays
- 1000–5000: momentum phase, size with volume trend
- Over 5000: you're late, CT play only if narrative is strong

### Dev History (via Helius wallet trace)
- Dev wallet rugged previously: HARD PASS, no exceptions
- Dev wallet distributed and stepped back: GREEN flag
- Dev wallet still holding >10%: YELLOW, reduce size
- Dev wallet swapped to SOL at launch: RED, pass

### Distribution Score
- Top 10 holders: ideally under 40% combined
- Any single wallet over 15%: reduce size
- Sniper concentration (same block buys): flag as high risk

### Smart Wallet Convergence
- 2 wallets: entry signal, 1–2 SOL probe
- 3 wallets: confirmed signal, 2–4 SOL
- 4 wallets: strong signal, 4–7 SOL
- 5 wallets: conviction call, up to 10 SOL if all other scores pass
